This is the first entry in **The Katalyst** journal — a running log of technical notes, product and design observations, and lessons from building software and startups.

Building a product involves much more than writing code. A useful feature starts with a question, takes shape through design, and changes as people try it. This journal is a place to look closely at that process, including the decisions that do not fit neatly into a finished project page.

Expect development notes, observations about information systems, and reflections on the relationship between design and software. Some entries will explore a specific interface or technical problem; others will consider what it means to build technology around the way people actually work.

The goal is to keep a thoughtful record of learning in progress. A rough idea can be worth sharing when it explains a choice, invites a better question, or helps someone else move forward.
